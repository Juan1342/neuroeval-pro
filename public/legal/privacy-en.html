Personal Data Processing Policy and Privacy Notice

Preliminary version for legal review. This document describes the intended processing and must be reviewed by Colombian counsel before being published as a final policy or used for an international launch.

Country: Colombia · Address: Carrera 60 # 71-10, Bello, Antioquia, Colombia
Privacy contact: soporte.neuroevalpro@gmail.com

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to NeuroEval Pro, its website, user accounts, guidance-oriented assessments, reports, support and administrative features. The controller determines the purposes and means of processing. For questions or claims, email the address above with the subject “Personal data protection”.

2. Data we may process

  • Account and identity: name, email, internal account identifier and basic profile details.
  • Assessment data: answers, scores, results, categories and reports concerning attention, organization, impulsivity, well-being and health-related matters. These may qualify as sensitive data.
  • Family observation: when an authorized adult uses the feature to observe a person aged 4–17, we may process the child’s stated age and derived age band, the adult’s relationship, observation settings and answers. Do not enter the child’s name, school or unnecessary identifying details.
  • Payments: transaction reference, status, amount, currency and reconciliation details received from Wompi. NeuroEval Pro does not request or store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Support and security: messages you submit, requests, technical records and security events needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Optional regional data: country, city, age range and language, only if you separately opt in to aggregated statistics.

3. Sensitive data and express consent

Answers and results related to health, attention or possible ADHD indicators may be sensitive data. Providing them is optional. Before an assessment begins, NeuroEval Pro will request prior, informed and express authorization separately from the general privacy notice. You may create an account and use non-assessment features without giving that authorization. If you decline or withdraw it, new assessments requiring those data will not begin; this does not automatically erase previously stored records.

For observations involving minors, the responding adult represents that they are a parent, legal representative or authorized caregiver, are entitled to provide the information, will act in the child’s best interests, explain the activity in an age-appropriate way and hear the child’s opinion in light of their maturity. Do not submit unnecessary identifying information. Adult consent does not replace any additional safeguards required by applicable law.

4. Purposes

  • Create and administer accounts, authenticate users and provide requested features.
  • Process answers and generate guidance-oriented self-report summaries.
  • Handle payments, reconciliation, support, security, fraud prevention and legal obligations.
  • Respond to access, correction, withdrawal and deletion requests.
  • Produce aggregated international reach statistics only with separate regional consent and suppress groups smaller than ten profiles. The map does not display individual locations and does not use GPS.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use individual results for targeted advertising. We do not make clinical diagnoses or automated decisions with legal effects on users.

5. Nature of the service

NeuroEval Pro is a guidance-oriented self-report tool. It does not provide medical care, make diagnoses, confirm or rule out ADHD, or replace professional assessment. Results should not be the sole basis for medical, educational, employment or other significant decisions.

6. Providers and international processing

We may use technology providers acting as processors or independent third parties depending on the service: Google/Firebase (authentication and database), Render (hosting), Wompi (payment processing) and Brevo (transactional email). Infrastructure may process or host information outside Colombia, including in Brazil and the United States. Each provider may apply its own terms, safeguards and processing locations. International transfers or transmissions will be handled under applicable legal requirements and safeguards. Review each provider’s privacy terms before using the service.

7. Security and access

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authentication, access permissions, encrypted communications, separation of duties and activity logs. Access to answers and reports is limited to the account holder and authorized personnel according to assigned permissions. No Internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, considering legal, contractual, accounting, tax and claims-defense obligations. Proposed operational rules: account data while the account remains active; answers and reports while the account remains active or another valid purpose exists; support tickets for up to 24 months after closure; recovery codes for 10 minutes; and accounting or payment records for the legally required period, which may reach ten years where applicable. Backups may retain copies until the provider’s technical cycle expires. Final periods and automated controls must be validated by legal and accounting advisers.

9. Your rights

You may request access, updates and corrections; proof of authorization; information about how data are used; submit a claim; withdraw consent and request deletion where no current legal or contractual duty requires retention. You may also contact the Colombian Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) after completing the applicable request procedure.

10. How to submit a request

You may also use Settings → Your data and privacy to download an available copy and submit a request. Alternatively, email soporte.neuroevalpro@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Include your name, the type of request (access, correction, withdrawal or deletion) and enough information to locate the record. We may request proportionate identity verification. Never send passwords, recovery codes or full card details. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable law. Deletion does not require removal of records subject to a current legal or contractual retention duty; in that case, use will be restricted to that purpose.

11. Cookies and local storage

The application uses browser local storage to remember preferences such as language and progress in certain activities on that device. You can clear progress with the “Delete progress on this device” button in Settings or through your browser; this does not delete records stored in your account.

12. Changes and contact

We will publish the current version and its date. Material changes will be communicated through reasonable means and, where required, a new authorization will be requested. Contact: soporte.neuroevalpro@gmail.com.

13. Legal references

This draft takes into account, among other references, Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing regulations compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015. Its application to the business model, minors’ data, the nature of the service and international data flows requires professional review.
